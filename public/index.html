01 — About the Institute

We do not merely study the future — we shape it.

"A strategic hub where rigorous research converges with purpose-driven innovation."

The Liberty Research Institute (LRI) is an independent research and innovation organisation founded in 2026. Our mission is to close the gap between discovery and measurable impact.

We were established on a simple principle: today's most pressing challenges require interdisciplinary rigour and real collaboration. Rather than publishing findings and walking away, LRI designs practical pathways that turn research into tangible outcomes — and trains the next generation of researchers, innovators, and policy leaders along the way.

03 — Publications

What we're publishing.

Our research is free to read and built to be used.

Working Paper No. 1
Integrating Artificial Intelligence into Nigeria's Healthcare System: A Comparative Analysis of Implementation Pathways, Benefits, Risks, and Regulatory Lessons from Seven Countries
Countries — Rwanda · Kenya · India · United Kingdom · China · United States · Nigeria

This research examines how Nigeria can integrate artificial intelligence into healthcare to improve health outcomes, access, and efficiency while addressing implementation and regulatory risks. Through a comparative analysis of seven countries, it identifies transferable lessons and proposes a phased roadmap for responsible, coordinated, and equitable AI adoption in Nigeria.

Funding & Independence

Liberty Research Institute is non-partisan and editorially independent. LRI does not accept funding conditioned on a predetermined research outcome, and no funder, donor, or partner is granted editorial control over findings, conclusions, or publications. Where a specific piece of research is funded by an external party, that support will be disclosed in the publication itself. LRI is currently in its founding funding round (Phase 1, 2026–2030); a public list of funders and partners will be published here as they are confirmed.
